# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

North Carolina pantry coordinators and community food-sharing participants are the primary audiences implied by the requested expiry tracker, neighborhood produce claims, and pantry-to-pantry exchange. The prototype also represents local growers and community food destinations in its sample boards. Their real operating roles and service areas have not been verified.

## Product Purpose

FoodCycle NC is a prototype for tracking pantry food by best-by date and exploring ways to route surplus to nearby people and organizations. Success for this prototype means a visitor can quickly identify what to use, update pantry stock, and demonstrate a produce claim or pantry transfer.

## Positioning

The requested product connects an at-home or pantry expiry list with community sharing and pantry-needs boards, instead of treating inventory expiration and redistribution as separate tasks. This is a prototype direction, not a validated market claim.

## Operating Context

The prototype is a browser-based demonstration for North Carolina communities. It includes illustrative locations around Raleigh, Durham, Cary, Hillsborough, Pittsboro, and Chapel Hill. Actions are demonstrated without an external service.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Track pantry items, quantities, storage locations, and best-by dates; filter and search the list; add, use, share, and remove items.
- Browse illustrative produce listings, filter by area, claim a quantity, and choose a sample pickup or delivery destination.
- Review sample pantry stock and needs, record a matching stock transfer, and record a notification action.
- Browser local storage persists inventory and demo-board actions on the current device. No account, backend, live availability, external messaging, or real transfer is provided.
- Broader farm-to-institution markets, emergency mobilization, volunteer-hours tracking, and impact dashboards are possible expansion ideas, not current prototype capabilities.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is FoodCycle NC. The requested identity is friendly, community-centered, distinctive, responsive, and accessible, with a North Carolina food-sharing context.

## Evidence on Hand

No real grower listings, partner organizations, service locations, inventory feeds, pickup schedules, client contacts, or impact measurements were provided. All names, quantities, destinations, and requests shown in the prototype are illustrative demo data and must be replaced with verified information before operational use.

## Product Principles

- Make soon-to-expire food easy to notice and act on.
- Keep adding, updating, and finding pantry items straightforward.
- Make surplus and unmet needs visible to neighbors.
- Label demonstration data and local-only actions honestly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The interface should remain usable with keyboard navigation and assistive technology, maintain readable contrast and clear action names, and adapt to narrow screens. Reduce motion when the user requests it.
